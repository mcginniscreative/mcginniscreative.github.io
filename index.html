About

I love telling stories through multimedia, especially when the work actually matters. For the past several years I've run McGinnis Creative, a one-person studio here in Rochester, where I've led full brand rebrands, built websites, and shot and edited video for clients ranging from a heating and cooling company to a youth sports camp.

Beyond client work, I've spent a lot of my career helping other people tell their own stories, whether it was my own students at Light House Studio, campers at Summer's Best 2 Weeks, students at my local church, or college students at Roberts Wesleyan University. Hearing those stories and making room for them to be shared has been just as important to me as telling my own.
